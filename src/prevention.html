Prevent & Protect Your Health

Small lifestyle changes can make a big difference.

Prevention Tips

Healthy Diet

Eat fruits, vegetables, and balanced meals to stay healthy.

Regular Exercise

Stay active to maintain a healthy body weight.

Routine Screening

Schedule regular medical check-ups and screenings.

Avoid Smoking

Smoking increases the risk of many cancers.

Limit Alcohol

Reduce alcohol intake to lower cancer risk.

Healthy Weight

Maintain a healthy weight through proper lifestyle habits.

Your Health Matters 🎗️

Prevention is always better than cure. Start making healthy choices today.

Stay Aware & Stay Safe

If you notice serious symptoms, visit the nearest hospital immediately.
