About Breast Cancer

Breast cancer is a disease where abnormal cells grow uncontrollably in the breast. It can begin in the milk ducts or lobules and may spread if not detected early. Learn more.

Symptoms of Breast cancer

Lump in Breast

Breast Pain

Change in Shape

Skin Dimpling

Self Examination Guide

Step 1: Mirror Check

Stand in front of a mirror and observe any visible change.

1

Step 2: Raise Arms

Lift your arms and check for any unusual shape or swelling.

2

Step 3: Feel for Lumps

Use your fingers to gently check for lumps and thick areas.

3

Step 4: Consult a Doctor

If you notice anything unusual, seek medical advice immediately.
