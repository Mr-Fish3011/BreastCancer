Self Breast Examination

Early detection starts with you. Check yourself regularly.

When to Check

Perform a self-exam once a month. The best time is 5 to 7 days after your period ends.

How to Perform a Self-Examination

1. Look in the Mirror

Stand in front of a mirror and check for any visible changes in size, shape, or skin.

2. Raise Your Arms

Raise your arms and check for the same changes from a different angle.

3. Check for Lumps

Use your fingers to feel for lumps or unusual thickening in the breast.

4. Lie Down & Examine

Lie down and check the entire breast area using a circular motion.

Helpful Tips

  • ✔ Use the pads of your fingers
  • ✔ Follow a consistent pattern
  • ✔ Check both breasts
  • ✔ Don’t panic if you find something — consult a doctor
