Recognize the Symptoms Early

Early detection of breast cancer can save lives. Learn the warning signs below.

Common Symptoms

Lump in Breast

A painless lump or thickening in the breast or underarm.

Change in Shape

Noticeable change in size or shape of the breast.

Skin Dimpling

Skin appears dimpled or like orange peel.

Nipple Discharge

Unusual discharge from the nipple.

Breast Pain

Persistent pain in the breast area.

Redness or Swelling

Inflammation or unusual swelling in the breast.

⚠️ Important Notice

If you notice any of these symptoms, consult a healthcare professional immediately. Early diagnosis greatly improves treatment outcomes.
